Privacy Policy

AdvisorReady (iOS and Android) · Last updated: 2026-11-27

AdvisorReady is an exam-preparation app. We built it to work without accounts, servers or tracking. This policy explains exactly what is and isn't collected.

What stays on your device

Your answers, scores, study history, review schedule and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's local database. We don't have servers that receive this data, and we can't see it. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Reset progress, deletes it.

What we don't collect

No account or sign-in. No name, email, phone number or location. No usage analytics, advertising SDKs, advertising identifiers or tracking across apps or websites.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. To confirm and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which processes an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and your purchase history (product, date and transaction identifiers). This information is not linked to your identity. It is used to provide and restore purchases, and to give us anonymous, aggregate sales reports (for example, how many unlocks were sold).

Contacting us

If you use "Report an issue" or email support, your email app sends us a message containing what you choose to include (for example, a question ID). We use it only to respond and to fix content, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for professional licensing exams and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the version in the app.
